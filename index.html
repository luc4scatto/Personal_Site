About

My path into 3D started at EssilorLuxottica, where I spent over four years as a 3D Render Specialist working on Prada Group, Burberry and Oliver Peoples eyewear lines.
In my last two years there I moved into improving and building new features for the 3D pipeline itself, a focus I carried forward into Thélios.
Today I'm part of the 3D R&D team at Thélios (LVMH eyewear), where I develop and maintain the 3D pipeline, from 2D design all the way to renders, 3D configurators and videos.
